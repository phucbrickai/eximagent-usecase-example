Questions

Can the quote match my brand?

Yes. Share your logo, colors, and an old quote you like, and Sales builds your template from them.

Who sends the quote?

You do. You read the draft and the team's notes, change anything you like, and send it yourself.

Where do the prices come from?

From the price list, cost sheet, and freight rates you give them. It works with the apps you already use.

Does it work for my product?

Yes. Coffee, rice, cashews, spices, fruit, seafood, or furniture follow the same steps with your own costs.
