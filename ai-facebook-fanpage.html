How it works

  1. Set the week’s goal

    A product update, a target market, or a trade fair.

  2. Review the drafts

    Posts, visuals, and inbox replies come back for your OK. Nothing is published or sent without it.

  3. Read the weekly report

    Reach, comments, and inquiries, on one page you can share with sales.

Questions

Does it post without my approval?

No. Posts and replies come back as drafts; you approve them before anything goes out.

What kind of posts does it create?

Product, shipment, and factory posts aimed at importers, each with a caption and a visual.

Can it answer buyer messages?

It drafts replies to common questions such as MOQ, specs, and lead time. You edit and send.

What is in the weekly report?

Reach, post views, buyer comments, and inquiries, plus the week’s top post.

Does this replace our marketing person?

No. It prepares the work so your people can review and decide.
