Questions

What is a website team?

A group of AI employees you hire to design, write, optimize, and report on your company website. They share one brain about your products and playbooks.

Do I have to use these four roles?

No. You hire the employees you need. These four are a starting team for a company website.

How do they know our products?

You add company knowledge once — products, profile, and playbooks — and assign it to the team. Every employee works from the same brain.

Can they work on a schedule?

Yes. Analytics can report every Monday, and other employees can run on the schedule you set.

Will this work with our existing tools?

Yes. The team works with the apps you already use, and every step goes to a work log.

Is this only for the first draft?

No. After the first pages are up, the team can keep updating copy, improving search, and reporting each week.
