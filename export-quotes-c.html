Questions

Who sends the quote?

You do. You read the draft and the team's notes, change anything you like, and send it yourself.

Where do the costs come from?

From the price list, cost sheet, and freight rates you give them. It works with the apps you already use.

Which Incoterms can they quote?

Any Incoterms 2020 term, written with a named place. For containers they can add FCA next to FOB or CIF.

What if the freight rate runs out before shipment?

Trade-Ops points it out and adds a freight clause, so you can keep it or ask the forwarder for a new rate.

Can I set up the team my own way?

Yes. You hire each employee, write their role and responsibilities, and choose who hands work to whom.

Does it work for products other than coffee?

Yes. Rice, cashews, spices, seafood, or furniture follow the same steps with your own costs and rates.
